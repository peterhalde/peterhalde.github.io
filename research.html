Research

I work on fair, reproducible benchmarking of vehicle controllers in simulation as well as real-world validation on EDGAR, TUM’s autonomous research vehicle. My recent work deals with developing a framework that compares controllers under identical scenarios, vehicle interfaces, and tuning conditions.

I am interested in doing research on learning-based controller tuning methods as well as efficient algorithm configuration.
