Research

How far can a car’s position estimate drift before its controller can no longer keep it on course? I use signal temporal logic to specify safety and tracking requirements, then increase disturbances until a controller first violates one. The disturbance it can tolerate gives me a measure of its robustness.

To check how well the simulation represents the car, I use measurements from EDGAR to model its response to steering and acceleration commands. I work with TIER IV on controller evaluation in Autoware Universe. I’m also interested in how to tune model predictive controllers automatically so that each gets a comparable tuning budget.
