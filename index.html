About me

I’m a doctoral researcher in the AV Evaluation team at the Chair of Automotive Technology at the Technical University of Munich. I study how to compare the controllers that steer autonomous vehicles and regulate their speed, both in city traffic and at high speeds.

A controller can look better in a test simply because it had more tuning time. I’m developing a benchmark that gives each controller the same driving scenarios, vehicle interface and tuning budget. I then check which results from simulation hold up on EDGAR, our research vehicle.
