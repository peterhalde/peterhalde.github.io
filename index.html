About me

I’m a doctoral researcher in the AV Evaluation team at the Chair of Automotive Technology at the Technical University of Munich. My research focusses on controller evaluation for autonomous vehicles, both in city traffic and at high speeds.

I am also the head supervisor of the lecture Autonomous Vehicles: Dynamics.
